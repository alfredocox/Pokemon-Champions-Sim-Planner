# OODA Cycle: Strategy Identity And Unsupported Advice

Candidate: v2.2.146-strategy-identity. Engine unchanged at 1.1.6.

## Observe

The audit reproduced stale reports after nature edits, cloned teams receiving the wrong report ID, and unsupported Fake Out/spread advice. Three new test groups failed before implementation while the previous 66 passed.

## Orient

Strategy signatures omitted stat inputs; report cache keys omitted registered identity. Mega cache keys omitted both teams' contents. Lead presence and team composition cannot prove a failed action or targeting mistake. These are shared analysis paths used by singles/doubles and user-authored teams.

## Decide

Use canonical complete inputs for cache lookup: registered team key, full team record, results, relevant opponent records, selected format, regulation registry, build and engine. Keep the existing bounded Strategy cache. Retain historical rule IDs but disable the two unsupported predicates until an event-backed replacement exists.

- Strategy lookup uses exact canonical inputs instead of short hash equality, preserving separate registrations and invalidating edited sets.
- Mega sweep lookup includes both full teams and execution context.
- Fake Out lead absence and spread-without-redirection no longer emit mistake claims.
- Focused Strategy suite: 69/69, including new both-format stat edits, cloned registration and Mega input change groups.
- Read-only audit diagnostic confirms nature no longer reuses a report, clone has its own key, and evidence-free Fake Out no longer fires.
