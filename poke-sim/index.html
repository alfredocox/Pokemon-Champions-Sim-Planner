How to use
1. Open your replay in Pokémon Showdown.
2. Copy the full replay log text, or save it as a plain .txt/.log file.
3. You can also paste a replay URL from replay.pokemonshowdown.com and let Battle Sensei load the raw log.
4. Paste it here or upload the replay file, choose your side, then run analysis.
Battle Sensei accepts raw logs, pasted replay-page text, replay URLs, and exported Showdown HTML. Screenshots and team exports are not replay inputs.
Optional roster completion only affects your selected side. In series play, Battle Sensei needs the registered six to judge why this game used this selected lineup.
Paste a log and run analysis to see result, leads, critical turn, coaching tags, and a readable turn timeline.
